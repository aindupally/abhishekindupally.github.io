Sector 1 Research

Teaching machines to read engineering geometry.

I apply deep and machine learning to 3D mechanical geometry and FEA data so design teams can explore, prototype and validate faster. The aim is tools that work alongside CAD and CAE, not in place of them.

  1. 1.1

    Generative AI for Engineering Design

    Building ML into real design workflows so designers can search constrained design spaces instead of iterating by hand.

  2. 1.2

    Geometric Representations for ML

    Encoding parts and assemblies as CAD features, meshes and graphs that learning models can use, while still reflecting load paths and manufacturability.

  3. 1.3

    Data-Driven & Physics-Based Surrogates

    Surrogates trained on FEA runs that predict stress, deflection and mass in milliseconds, with physics constraints that keep predictions plausible.

  4. 1.4

    AI Foundations for Computational Design

    Bringing geometry-aware models, surrogates and optimization together into one product development loop.

Currently on track: deep learning on automotive hood frame geometries that learns how shape drives structural performance, for near-instant surrogate prediction.

Sector 2 The Garage

Projects, built and tested.

#01IDETC-CIE 2026 · Best Paper

Bridging CAD and Data-Driven Design: Attributed Feature Graphs for Engineering Design

Introduces Attributed Feature Graphs (AFGs): CAD features such as extrusions, ribs and pockets become nodes with parametric attributes, linked by geometric and dependency edges. A GNN trained on the CarHoods10K hood-frame dataset predicts stress, mass and deflection, and every prediction traces back to an editable CAD feature.

  • 0.89R² mass
  • 0.87R² stress & deflection
  • ~9.5%MAPE (CNN baselines 8–15%)
  • 93.6%within 20% of FEA

With Dr. Ibraheem Alawadhi, Dr. Jami J. Shah (Ohio State) & Dr. Satchit Ramnath (Clemson)

PyTorch Geometric · NetworkX · CAD regeneration

#02IDETC 2025 · First author

Multimodal ML for Performance Prediction

Combines images, cross-sections and parametric features with geometric context to predict hood-frame performance on CarHoods10K, cutting how often full FEA runs are needed. Outperforms an image-only baseline, especially for stress and deflection, and stays physically plausible on unseen designs.

  • <20%val. error, stress / mass / deflection

Optimizing Surface Roughness in Electrodeposition

Nickel electrodeposition study combining COMSOL Multiphysics, Taguchi methods and simulated annealing, which reduced surface roughness (Δy = 0.258) and defects.

#05Materials · FEA

Material Selection for Lightweight Fire-Extinguisher Levers

Screened titanium alloys, silicon nitride and CFRP against a 75 lb actuation load, E ≥ 65 GPa and a –65 °F to 120 °F operating range. 409 stainless steel gave the best balance of strength, cost, manufacturability and recyclability.

#06Additive · DOE

3D-Printed Brake Pedal via DOE & ASTM Testing

Tensile, flexural and impact testing to ASTM standards across infill pattern, raster angle, layer thickness, material and infill density. Infill density and material mattered most. The final pedal used generative design and reverse engineering.
